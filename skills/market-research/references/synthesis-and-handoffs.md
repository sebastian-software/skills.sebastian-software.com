# Synthesis and Handoffs

Use synthesis to preserve what the evidence says, where it came from, how it
varies, and which owner may act on it. Do not turn a neat theme into certainty.

## Build the evidence register

For each consequential item record:

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Evidence ID | Stable identifier |
| Source | Session, record, dataset, URL, or document |
| Date and context | Observation and relevant operating conditions |
| Person or unit | Role, segment, product, cohort, or market unit |
| Evidence type | Observation, account, behavior, commitment, metric, public fact, or interpretation |
| Exact material | Quote, event, value, excerpt, or calculation |
| Selection mechanism | How this material entered the corpus |
| Limits | Bias, missing context, access, freshness, or measurement issue |
| Affected decision | Product, design, market, message, price, or next research |

Keep identifiers and sensitive raw material outside broadly shared synthesis
artifacts when the decision does not require them.

## Move through explicit layers

Use:

```text
raw material -> observations -> patterns -> interpretation
             -> implications -> owner decision
```

An observation stays close to what happened or was said. A pattern connects
multiple observations without claiming prevalence beyond the corpus. An
interpretation proposes meaning. An implication states what an owner should
examine. The owner makes the product, design, positioning, or pricing decision.

Code at the smallest useful level. Retain source links while grouping related
observations. Split themes that hide meaningful role, segment, alternative, or
outcome differences. Preserve outliers and negative cases that challenge the
dominant explanation.

## Report strength without score theater

Judge a finding by:

- directness to the decision
- source and method quality
- relevant participant or unit coverage
- recurrence across independent evidence
- consistency across or difference between important segments
- support from observed behavior or commitment
- contradicting evidence and plausible alternatives
- freshness and transfer to the target context

Do not use a universal “three sources equals high confidence” rule or average
different weaknesses into a reassuring score. State confidence in prose tied to
the evidence, and name what could disconfirm the finding.

When reporting frequency, give the actual denominator and corpus:
“6 of 11 interviewed administrators described a manual approval workaround” is
bounded evidence. “Most customers need approval automation” is not supported
unless the sample and measurement justify that population claim.

## Produce a Voice of Customer bank

For every quotation preserve:

- exact wording
- speaker or source identifier appropriate to consent and access
- role, segment, situation, and trigger
- question or event that elicited it
- product, alternative, or outcome context
- date and permission or usage restriction
- theme and intended internal use

Do not combine fragments into a synthetic quote, polish grammar without marking
the change, or publish research material as a testimonial without the required
permission and review.

## Describe jobs and behavioral patterns

A useful Jobs to Be Done statement includes:

- triggering situation and prior state
- desired functional, emotional, and social progress
- current alternatives and tradeoffs
- anxieties, habits, switching costs, and constraints
- evidence supporting and contradicting the pattern

Create a persona only when a durable behavioral pattern helps a team make a
different decision. Base it on goals, context, strategies, roles, constraints,
expectations, and meaningful variations. Avoid decorative biography,
unsupported demographics, cute names, and a fictional average across segments.
Label provisional or proxy-based personas and define what evidence should
replace them.

## Connect findings to owners

### Product Management

Return:

- target situations, triggers, jobs, alternatives, and commitments
- repeated value, switching, adoption, retention, and failure evidence
- market access, authority, viability inputs, and important unknowns
- product-thesis, scope, quality, or distribution assumptions challenged

`product-management` decides target market, product thesis, outcomes, scope,
priorities, roadmap, release, and product experiments.

### Product Design

Return:

- situated behaviors, tools, artifacts, environments, and interruptions
- workarounds, mental-model clues, expectations, accessibility needs, and risk
- sequence, coordination, state, permission, error, and recovery observations
- contrasting patterns and realistic tasks for modeling or prototyping

`product-design` decides problem framing, design principles, objects,
relationships, flows, interaction models, prototypes, and usability response.

### Product Marketing

Return:

- buyer, user, payer, approver, blocker, and channel context
- category and alternative language
- evaluation criteria, objections, triggers, win/loss sequence, and proof gaps
- attributable customer vocabulary and publication constraints

`product-marketing` decides segment, category, positioning, message, claims,
proof, launch, sales enablement, and channel learning.

### Pricing and Packaging

Return the value situations, buying roles, alternatives, usage or outcome
measures, budget process, switching constraints, and observed commercial
commitments. `pricing-and-packaging` owns willingness-to-pay method, value
metric, price, packages, discounts, trials, and migrations.

## Deliver a decision-ready synthesis

Use the smallest artifact that supports the decision:

1. decision contract and evidence cutoff
2. corpus, sample, sources, methods, exclusions, and access limits
3. evidence register and raw-material locations
4. findings with supporting and contradicting evidence
5. meaningful segment or role differences
6. confidence limits and alternative explanations
7. owner-specific implications and open decisions
8. unresolved questions and next research trigger
9. retention, deletion, and repository obligations

Keep a research repository usable by preserving stable IDs, versions, dates,
source access, decision links, and superseded findings. Do not silently rewrite
an old synthesis when later evidence changes the conclusion.
