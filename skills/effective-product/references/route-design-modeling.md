## Apply

- Design the system before drawing screens. Identify actors, objects, rules,
  relationships, state, permissions, and consequences.
- Explore more than one structural or interaction direction. Compare agency,
  comprehension, consequence, accessibility, implementation constraints, and fit
  with the product outcome.
- Prototype only the fidelity needed to answer the next question. Test with
  realistic tasks, content, states, and interruptions before increasing craft.
- Make consequential state visible and actions recoverable.
