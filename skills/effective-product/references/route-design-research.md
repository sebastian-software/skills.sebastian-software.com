## Apply

- Add an activity because it reduces a named uncertainty, not because a diagram
  says it comes next.
- Keep observations, participant language, interpretation, design principles,
  opportunities, and solution ideas in distinguishable layers. A repeated quote
  is not a feature requirement.
- Prefer empowerment framing: what people are trying to achieve and what helps
  them exercise agency, not only what is broken.
- Never invent participants, observations, quotes, or usability findings. With
  no evidence, deliver a plan, a hypothesis, or a labeled provisional model.
