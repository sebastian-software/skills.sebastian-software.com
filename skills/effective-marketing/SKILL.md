# Effective Marketing

Take verified value to market. Turn stable product, customer, and career
evidence into a position people understand, claims they can trust, copy that
helps a decision, channels that reach the right reader, and a learning loop that
can revise all of it.

Keep the layers separately reviewable:

```text
product evidence -> market position -> message expression -> channel tactic
```

Never use polished copy or channel response as retroactive proof that the
product works or that the position is true.

## Workflow

1. Name the decision or artifact, the audience, and the one next action it
   should produce.
2. Establish the evidence boundary before writing anything. Separate verified
   product behavior, observed customer situations and language, dated market
   and competitor facts, and interpretation or assumption.
3. Select one primary route from the table. Read that route before acting.
4. Load only the references the route names for this task.
5. Produce the smallest reviewable artifact. Keep strategy, copy,
   implementation, assumptions, and tests separable.
6. Name the evidence gaps, the confidence the recommendation actually carries,
   and what would reopen the decision.

Never invent customer quotations, testimonials, outcomes, statistics, market
size, competitor behavior, guarantees, scarcity, deadlines, or differentiation.
Mark material gaps or work within them honestly.

## Operating Rules

- Start from a recognizable customer situation and current alternative, not a
  demographic label, an imagined persona, or a theatrical pain point.
- Make every consequential outcome, comparison, speed, ease, universality, or
  superiority claim traceable to current evidence and material constraints.
  Preserve the qualifications that keep a claim true.
- Use proof proportional to the claim and audience risk.
- Use customer language only when its source and context are known. Never
  synthesize fragments into a quotation or testimonial.
- Use psychological mechanisms as testable design choices, not as permission to
  exploit vulnerability. Reject fake urgency, hidden costs, obstructed refusal,
  deceptive defaults, shame, and synthetic social proof.
- Treat accessibility as a conversion requirement and a guardrail. A change that
  excludes people is not an optimization.
- Keep platform mechanics, cadence numbers, limits, algorithm claims, and policy
  details volatile. Verify current official guidance before making one a
  requirement, and frame tactics as hypotheses to test.
- Optimize for qualified conversations and commercial learning, not impressions,
  follower count, or connection volume.
- Drafting does not authorize publishing, scheduling, messaging, or other
  account mutations. Act externally only on an explicit request.
- Do not promise conversion. A recommendation is a hypothesis until observed
  evidence supports a causal conclusion.

## Routing Boundaries

- Route new customer or buyer research — recruitment, interviews, surveys,
  observation, win/loss fieldwork, review and community mining, market sizing,
  cross-source synthesis — to `effective-product`. Pass the decision and
  evidence gaps; receive buyer language, alternatives, decision criteria, proof
  gaps, and evidence limits without delegating the market-facing decision.
- Route target user, customer problem, product thesis, scope, quality bar,
  release decision, and all pricing, packaging, discount, trial, entitlement,
  and customer-migration choices to `effective-product`. This discipline
  communicates only approved commercial facts and their evidence limits. Return
  market response and adoption evidence there when it changes the product
  decision.
- Route durable positioning, audience, voice, tone, terminology, and
  cross-channel communication decisions to `effective-product` for recording as
  an Architecture Decision Record.
- Route articles, essays, newsletters, thought leadership, editorial case
  studies, public project narratives, and other primarily informative or
  argumentative long-form prose to `effective-writing` once the audience,
  position, claims, proof, purpose, and terminology are stable enough to brief.
  This discipline owns moving a reader through a commercial choice; that one
  owns informing and explaining.
- Route locale-specific punctuation, quotation, spacing, number, date, and
  currency conventions, and any evidence-based audit of formulaic or AI-sounding
  prose, to `effective-writing`.
- Route page hierarchy, interface copy, forms, analytics delivery, experiment
  implementation, accessibility, performance, responsive behavior, and browser
  verification to `effective-web`.
- Route jurisdiction-specific requirements for claims, testimonials,
  endorsements, direct marketing, privacy, consent, tracking, disclosures,
  subscriptions, and online sales to `effective-web`.
- Route comparison of a produced campaign or market-facing experience against
  supplied references for copied identity, content, claims, media, or
  distinctive combinations to `effective-web`; return here to correct the
  approved position, message, proof, and campaign brief.
- Route repository-derived technical behavior, executable examples, and
  controlled-language requirements to `effective-delivery`; consume the verified
  facts, terminology, and constraints without handing it the channel voice.
- This discipline does not authorize deceptive interfaces, manufacture
  research, implement browser changes, or replace legal, privacy, or specialist
  statistical review when risk and uncertainty exceed the available evidence.
