# Qualify Before a Full Sales Conversation

## Purpose

Qualification determines whether a longer consultation is useful to both sides.
A full calendar of poor-fit calls consumes customer and sales time. Define the
minimum standard for each offer before evaluating individual leads.

| Area | Question to resolve |
| --- | --- |
| Problem | What is happening now? |
| Impact | What does it cost or block? |
| Goal | What change is wanted? |
| Timing | Why is this relevant now? |
| Decision | Who must participate and authorize spending? |
| Resources | Are financial and operational resources plausible? |
| Participation | Can the customer meet the service's prerequisites? |

Five conditions should be plausible before a full sales meeting: real need,
customer and organizational fit, financial and operational room, the right
decision participants, and realistic timing. “I don't know yet” remains an open
point; it is not automatically a pass or a failure. Ask open questions, clarify
vague answers, and record figures only when the prospect actually provides them.

## Short phone guide

```text
Reason: “I'd like to see whether a conversation about [topic]
would be useful for you.”
Current state: “How does [process] work today?”
Problem: “Where does it create work, loss, or frustration?”
Goal: “What would need to change for this to be resolved?”
Timing: “Why are you looking at it now?”
Decision and resources: “Who would be involved, and what resources
have you considered?”
Participation: “What could your team contribute?”
Decision: Book, set a meaningful later check-in, or close politely.
```

Explain why a sensitive question is relevant. A rigid checklist should not push
someone into a claim they cannot substantiate.

## Define exclusions from evidence

Review actual poor-fit calls for repeated signals: absent authority, missing
essential prerequisite, a fundamentally different goal, or low priority. Make
criteria observable and test whether they predict delivery and sales outcomes.
Vague judgments about a person's character are not useful qualification. Any
scoring system should combine explicit business signals only.

## Handoff and feedback

For a good-fit lead, book a timely meeting and state its purpose, duration, and
preparation. Record problem, goal, timing, participants, resources, objections,
open questions, and next step in the CRM. For a poor fit, give a factual reason
and offer an alternative only if it is genuinely helpful. Do not relabel a
disqualified lead as a sales appointment.

If poor-fit inquiries recur, examine the audience, ad, form, and message. If
no-shows recur, inspect booking delay, expectations, and confirmation. Compare
rates only across similar segments and periods.
