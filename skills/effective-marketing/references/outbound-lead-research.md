# Research Qualified Leads

## Define the segment

Specify industry or use case, location where relevant, company size, maturity,
buyer role, visible signs of the problem, and exclusions. A narrow segment makes
the first message more relevant. If suitable prospects cannot be identified
reliably, consider an inbound path instead of list-based outreach.

## Five-step research process

1. **Choose a core segment:** Find the overlap of urgent problem, ability to
   buy, deliverability, and access. Industry plus size plus trigger may be
   enough.
2. **Understand the economics:** Research how these firms earn revenue, who owns
   the problem, and what constraints they face. Do not assume every company in a
   category has the same need.
3. **Find relevant changes:** Industry developments, regulations, technology,
   and market shifts can create a timely reason to speak. Verify each claim
   before using it.
4. **Collect appropriate data:** Choose directories or data vendors for the
   fields actually needed: company, role, business contact channel, or address.
   No provider is equally good at every field.
5. **Pre-filter:** Manually review a sample, remove duplicates and poor fits,
   then import. Check local rules and source terms for the proposed use.

Possible sources include company sites, search results, public directories,
event lists, referrals, and specialist vendors. A purchased list still requires
verification. Record only information needed to assess fit and conduct the
approved process. Source fields belong in the internal working record, not
automatically in the message.

## Working-list schema

```text
Company | website | industry/use case | market | size band
Buyer role / named contact if verified | permitted contact channel
Why this account fits | visible relevance signal | exclusion reason
Source and research date | first attempt | last buyer contact
Status | owner | next action | follow-up date
```

Treat missing data as unknown. Do not invent personalization from an industry
label. For larger lists, process a small segment first and measure error rates.
On import, check encoding, field mapping, duplicates, and permitted use.

## Quality and coverage

Track valid records, actual buyer contacts, recognized need, qualified meetings,
and eventual customers separately. **List coverage = unique decision makers
reached / eligible accounts on the active list.** Also track qualified
conversations per buyer reached, record-error rate, and active records without a
next action. Repeated calls to a few familiar contacts can create activity while
most of the list remains untouched. Update segment criteria from real
conversations and version important changes.
