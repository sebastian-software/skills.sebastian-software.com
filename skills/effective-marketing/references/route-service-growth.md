# Route: Service Business Growth

Use this route for an end-to-end service-business system or when the constraint
is still unknown: starting a service business, finding the current bottleneck
from offer through acquisition, sales, and delivery, choosing the next few
actions, weekly planning, and delegable procedures.

## Read

- [Service growth operations](service-growth-operations.md) — startup phases,
  business setup checklist, bottleneck map, one-week decision sheet, weekly
  planning, board, and delegable procedures.
- [Service offer](service-offer.md) — when the bottleneck is the offer itself.
- [Content journey and topic bank](content-journey-and-topics.md) — when the
  bottleneck is visibility: primary job per piece, journey stage, topic bank
  from customer conversations, and learning from outcomes. Drafting the pieces
  continues in **Social** or **LinkedIn posts**.

## Apply

- Map suitable people reached → conversations → qualified opportunities →
  sales → good delivery → referrals or repeat work. Check data quality first,
  then process and message, and mark the weakest transition.
- Begin at the actual constraint: offer before messaging, conversion path
  before CRM automation, delivery before more demand. For an end-to-end system,
  work from offer through acquisition and sales to delivery and measurement.
- Change one major assumption at a time and state a testable hypothesis with an
  observable signal and review date.
- Deliver a usable result with a next action, owner, and observable success
  signal. In diagnosis, separate observation, possible cause, and test.
- Establish the target country or region, B2B or consumer context, customer
  behavior, economics, delivery capacity, and available proof before
  recommending a channel. Do not transplant a tactic, threshold, price, or
  script across markets without testing its assumptions.
