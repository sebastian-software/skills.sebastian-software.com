# Route: Paid Campaigns

Use this route for planning, launching, and diagnosing paid lead campaigns:
local campaigns, creative angles, lead paths, retargeting, and campaign
measurement from spend to sale. Start from offer, audience, result event, and
economic constraints.

## Read

- [Local campaigns](paid-local-campaigns.md) — geographic relevance, campaign
  brief, creative openings, contact path, and prelaunch acceptance.
- [Retargeting](paid-retargeting.md) — permitted prior signals, audience and
  message, technical sequence, and small audiences.
- [Campaign measurement](paid-measurement.md) — the metric chain, diagnosis in
  sequence, and the one-page decision report.

## Apply

- Establish market, customer, budget, contact path, proof, and lead-handling
  capacity. Set budget and geographic range from market size, delivery capacity,
  and observed data, not from a borrowed example.
- Define an event that represents genuine demand, such as a valid inquiry or a
  confirmed booking, and connect the campaign source to the CRM.
- Align creative, copy, page or form, and follow-up. Avoid false scarcity,
  unsupported superlatives, and forms that promise something the destination
  does not deliver.
- Platform screens, policies, tracking, and privacy rules change and differ by
  market. Check current official guidance before changing settings.
- Keep variants interpretable and evaluate qualified meetings and sales
  alongside platform metrics.
