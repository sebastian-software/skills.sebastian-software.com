# Route: Outbound Prospecting

Use this route for B2B prospect research, list building, cold email, phone
outreach, direct mail with follow-up, opt-in video follow-up, and events. Begin
with customer fit and an offer that has some evidence behind it. A useful lead
has a plausible problem, a relevant role, and a reachable next step.

## Apply

- Establish the recipient's jurisdiction, business or consumer status, source
  of the contact data, relationship, and lawful contact basis before planning a
  campaign. Rules for phone, email, direct messages, and personal data differ by
  market and change; check current official guidance for the actual market. A
  script does not make an impermissible contact acceptable.
- Define inclusion and exclusion criteria, build a checkable list, and choose a
  channel by segment precision, relevance, cost, and the team's capacity to
  answer. Start with one channel the team can run and measure reliably.
- Give every contact a truthful, verified reason and a low-friction first step.
  Do not imply a prior relationship, fabricate personalization, or disguise a
  pitch as an administrative or research question.
- Respect objections, refusals, and opt-outs promptly and record them in a
  reliable suppression process.
- Judge outbound by qualified conversations, meetings, and opportunities per
  segment, not by send or call volume.
