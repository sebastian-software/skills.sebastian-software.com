# Route: Consultative Sales

Use this route for turning messages into qualified conversations, qualification
before a full meeting, the consultation itself, the price conversation,
objections, role-play, follow-up, and expansion or referral conversations with
existing clients. Support an informed decision: check need and fit before
recommending an offer, and learn from documented outcomes.

## Apply

- Establish the offer, buyer, conversation stage, and known facts. Set the
  purpose and questions for this stage; build a guide with branches rather than
  a fixed speech.
- Answer price questions honestly with the known price or a transparent range
  and its scope. Change price only with a reasoned change in scope, schedule,
  payment terms, or risk allocation.
- Treat refusal, satisfaction with an existing provider, and missing fit as
  legitimate outcomes. Do not manufacture urgency, dissatisfaction, or
  return-on-investment figures.
- For a role-play, simulate a credible buyer and give concrete feedback
  afterward. For a real review, separate observation, interpretation, and the
  next experiment.
- Before replying in someone's voice, read the full thread. Use call recordings
  only with an appropriate basis.
