# Run Sales Work in the CRM

## Role handoffs

Prospecting, first contact, qualification, sales, and delivery may be owned by
one person or several. Every handoff needs the reason for contact, actual
response, problem, qualification status, next action, and owner. Activity alone
should not move an opportunity to a later stage.

| Handoff | Minimum context |
| --- | --- |
| Research → first contact | Source, fit reason, channel, relevant observation, prior attempts |
| First contact → qualification | Person reached, expressed interest, constraints, agreed next step |
| Qualification → sales | Problem, goal, previous attempt, timing, decision participants, open questions |
| Sales → delivery | Promised scope, timeline, price, customer contribution, risks |

The receiving role confirms the information is usable.

## Saved views with action rules

| View | Filter | Required action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New and untouched | Intake without documented attempt | Assign owner and first contact |
| Due today | Open task due today or overdue | Complete or reschedule with reason |
| Reached without a decision | Conversation with no next step | Ask follow-up question or close |
| Qualified without a meeting | Fit confirmed, no date | Offer concrete meeting options |
| Upcoming meetings | Booked and not canceled | Prepare, send material or reminder |
| Missed meetings | No-show or move without new date | Check interest and document reason |
| Open proposals | Offer discussed, decision pending | Honor agreed follow-up |
| Unowned opportunities | Active record without owner | Assign and set due action |
| Won, awaiting handoff | Commitment without delivery start | Transfer scope and promises |

Give each view an owner, review rhythm, and escalation rule. Verify filters
against a real record. A view nobody checks becomes stale.

## Daily and weekly rhythm

Start with due tasks and new inquiries. After every attempt, record result and
next action immediately. At day's end, inspect open records without follow-up.
Weekly, review stage transitions: Where are opportunities accumulating? Which
exclusion reasons recur? Which tasks are overdue? Change rules based on actual
records rather than anecdotes.

After a call, note outcome, essential facts, objection, promised information,
next step, and date. Long unstructured notes without a decision are hard to use.
Record why a lead is disqualified so marketing can learn.

## Define reporting terms before comparison

**Contact rate = people reached / valid attempts.** **Meeting rate = booked
meetings / qualified conversations.** **Attendance rate = attended / booked
meetings.** **Close rate = won opportunities / completed sales conversations.**
A company may use different denominators; document them. Always show counts,
period, segment, channel, and role. Check data completeness before attributing a
weak result to a person, message, or channel.
