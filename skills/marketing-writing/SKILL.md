# Marketing Writing

Turn approved market truth into copy that earns attention, makes a useful
choice easier to understand, and supports action without manufacturing desire,
certainty, proof, or urgency. Treat copywriting, marketing psychology, and copy
editing as one traceable workflow rather than three disconnected tricks.

## Workflow

1. Define the assignment:
   - artifact, channel, format, length, language, and voice
   - commercial goal and one primary next action
   - reader, entry context, existing knowledge, and decision stage
   - supplied source copy, brief, evidence, constraints, and approval status
   - mode: brief, outline, draft, rewrite, focused edit, critique, or variants
2. Establish the truth boundary. Separate:
   - verified product behavior, offer, pricing, eligibility, and constraints
   - approved position, claims, differentiators, proof, and terminology
   - observed customer situations, alternatives, objections, and exact language
   - hypotheses, stakeholder preferences, missing evidence, and prohibited
     claims

   Never invent customer quotations, testimonials, outcomes, statistics,
   guarantees, scarcity, deadlines, product behavior, or unsupported
   differentiation. Mark material gaps or write within them honestly.
3. Read [Brief and reader state](references/brief-and-reader-state.md) when the
   input is incomplete, multiple audiences or traffic sources compete, or the
   copy must match a particular buying situation.
4. Choose the smallest persuasive path that fits the decision. Read
   [Persuasive paths and artifacts](references/persuasive-paths-and-artifacts.md)
   for belief order, claim-and-proof placement, calls to action, and adaptation
   across pages, campaigns, launches, and emails. Do not force every assignment
   into one universal formula.
5. Read [Emotion and behavior](references/emotion-and-behavior.md) when the work
   must create recognition, anticipation, relief, confidence, identity, or
   momentum; when a psychological principle is proposed; or when persuasion
   risks becoming pressure or a dark pattern.
6. Build the reading path before polishing sentences. Give each section one
   decision job, place proof near the claim it supports, surface consequential
   constraints before commitment, and make the next step concrete.
7. Draft with the reader's situation and vocabulary. Translate capabilities
   through mechanism into credible progress. Blend narrative, explanation, and
   evidence in the proportions the offer, audience, risk, and channel require.
8. Read [Marketing copy revision](references/marketing-copy-revision.md) for an
   existing draft, a copy critique, or the final quality gate. Revise in
   separate passes so stronger emotion or brevity does not weaken truth,
   meaning, voice, or necessary qualifications.
9. Return the requested artifact plus only the evidence gaps, consequential
   choices, or materially different variants needed for review. Do not bury
   finished copy beneath a generic lecture about copywriting.

## Operating Rules

- Start from a recognizable reader situation, desired progress, current
  alternative, or unresolved decision—not a theatrical pain point.
- Prefer specific, concrete language to empty intensity. Keep approved
  technical terms when they help the intended reader decide accurately.
- Connect feature, mechanism, consequence, and value without treating a
  plausible benefit as a measured outcome.
- Use customer language only when its source and context are known. Do not
  synthesize several fragments into a fake quotation or testimonial.
- Make emotional resonance truthful: evoke the real stakes, effort, identity,
  uncertainty, anticipation, or relief already present in the situation.
- Use psychological mechanisms as testable design choices, not as a catalog of
  biases or permission to exploit vulnerability.
- Keep proof proportional and adjacent to consequential claims. Preserve
  sample, period, segment, conditions, and uncertainty when they affect truth.
- Treat clarity and creativity as contextual, not opposing absolutes. A vivid
  line is useful when it sharpens recognition or memory without obscuring the
  offer.
- Give the primary action visual and verbal priority while allowing secondary
  paths that serve genuinely different reader needs.
- Reject fake urgency, hidden costs, obstructed refusal, deceptive defaults,
  shame, synthetic social proof, and fear that outruns the evidence.
- When editing, distinguish correction from preference. Preserve deliberate
  voice, rhythm, terminology, and qualifications unless they obstruct the
  assignment.
- Do not promise conversion. Describe variants and recommendations as
  hypotheses until observed evidence supports a causal conclusion.

## Default Deliverable

For new copy, return:

1. the finished copy organized for its artifact;
2. essential annotations for claim, proof, audience, or sequence choices;
3. unresolved evidence or approval gaps;
4. alternatives only where the choice materially changes reader effect.

For revision or critique, return:

1. the revised copy or prioritized diagnosis requested;
2. the most consequential message, proof, emotion, and action changes;
3. facts, qualifications, or owner decisions that remain unresolved.

## Routing Boundaries

- Use `market-research` to gather or synthesize customer situations, buyer
  language, alternatives, objections, decision criteria, and proof needs. This
  skill writes from supplied evidence; it does not manufacture Voice of
  Customer or declare market prevalence.
- Use `product-marketing` to select the audience, position, category, message
  hierarchy, claims, proof, launch strategy, and channel purpose. Receive an
  approved writing brief here; return material message or proof gaps without
  silently changing the position.
- Use `pricing-and-packaging` to decide the value metric, price, package,
  discount, trial, guarantee policy, eligibility, and migration. This skill
  expresses only approved commercial facts and constraints.
- Use `nonfiction-writing` when the primary job is to inform, explain, argue,
  teach, report, or demonstrate expertise through an article, essay,
  newsletter, thought-leadership piece, or editorial case study. Use this skill
  when the primary job is to move a reader through a commercial choice.
- Use `effective-web` to decide page hierarchy and interface behavior; write
  labels, errors, states, and other interface copy; implement the page; and
  verify accessibility, responsiveness, performance, and browser behavior.
- Use `conversion-optimization` to diagnose a funnel constraint, design a
  learning method, and judge results. This skill drafts or revises the approved
  copy intervention without treating its recommendation as measured uplift.
- Use `create-social-content`, `linkedin-posts`, or `linkedin-social-selling`
  for platform-native distribution, cadence, engagement, relationship, and
  pipeline behavior after the underlying message and evidence are stable.
- Use `web-legal-compliance` for current jurisdiction-specific requirements
  affecting claims, testimonials, endorsements, disclosures, direct marketing,
  consent, tracking, subscriptions, and online sales.
- Use `locale-typography` for locale-specific punctuation, quotation, spacing,
  number, date, currency, and typographic conventions.
