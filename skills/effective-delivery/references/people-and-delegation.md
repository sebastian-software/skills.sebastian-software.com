# People and Delegation

Use this reference for one-to-ones, coaching, feedback, delegation, and
professional growth. Formal employment action remains outside this skill.

## One-to-Ones

The report should own much of the agenda. Useful areas include:

- current context and obstacles;
- feedback in both directions;
- decisions or relationships needing support;
- growth interests and opportunities;
- workload, focus, and sustainable pace.

Do not use the meeting for routine status extraction that another channel can
serve. Record agreed actions with appropriate privacy; do not create a hidden
dossier.

## Choose Direction, Coaching, or Delegation

- **Direct** when safety, urgency, regulation, or a narrow mandate leaves little
  room for exploration. Explain the constraint and duration.
- **Coach** when the person owns the decision and reflection can improve their
  judgment. Ask concrete questions about outcome, evidence, options, and risks.
- **Delegate** when the person can own a bounded result with real authority,
  context, resources, and feedback.

Do not coach theatrically when the decision has already been made. Do not
delegate a problem while withholding the permissions or relationships needed
to solve it.

## Write a Delegation Contract

State:

1. outcome and why it matters;
2. decision space and explicit exclusions;
3. constraints, commitments, and relevant context;
4. resources and collaborators;
5. escalation triggers;
6. checkpoint frequency based on risk and experience;
7. definition of completion and learning review.

Increase autonomy through demonstrated judgment and shared context, not through
silence. A checkpoint should surface risk and learning, not covertly reapprove
every step.

## Give Usable Feedback

Describe:

- the specific observable behavior or result;
- the context in which it occurred;
- its impact on the work or people;
- the other person's perspective and missing context;
- the requested or jointly designed next behavior;
- follow-up and support.

Separate facts from interpretation. Deliver consequential feedback privately,
promptly enough to act, and without diagnosing motive or personality.

## Support Growth

Connect growth to meaningful work:

- identify a capability and evidence of the current level;
- select a stretch responsibility with bounded risk;
- provide access, sponsorship, feedback, and time;
- review both the result and the reasoning learned.

Do not promise promotions, compensation, or roles outside the manager's
authority. Make sponsorship and opportunity allocation visible enough to audit
for inequity.

## Handle Sensitive Concerns

Stop and involve qualified owners when the matter concerns harassment,
discrimination, retaliation, employment status, compensation disputes,
medical information, acute safety, or crisis. Preserve privacy, do not
investigate beyond authority, and do not promise a particular outcome.

## Deliverable

Return the conversation purpose, evidence, chosen management mode, shared
contract or request, support, privacy boundary, and review point.
