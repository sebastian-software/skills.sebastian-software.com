# Route: Persuasive Nonfiction

Use this route for thought leadership, content marketing, editorial case
studies, professional narratives, objections, proof, and proportionate calls to
action — persuasion carried by editorial prose rather than by a commercial page.

## Read

1. [Nonfiction assignment, evidence, and revision](nonfiction-assignment.md) —
   the shared contract, including the evidence register.
2. [Persuasive nonfiction](persuasive-nonfiction.md) — the route's working
   guidance.

## Apply

- Make persuasive intent legible. Relevance, proof, fair treatment of
  uncertainty, and an appropriate next step do the work; inflated claims, fake
  urgency, and disguised advertising do not.
- Anticipate the reader's strongest objection and answer it rather than routing
  around it.
- Keep the call to action proportionate to what the piece earned.
- Never invent testimonials, customer stories, measured outcomes, or citations.
